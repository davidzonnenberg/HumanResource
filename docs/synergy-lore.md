# Synergy: story notes (wishlist, not built)

Synergy is the group that seems nice at first and turns out to be a cult. Nothing here is a requirement. It is a pool of text and beats to draw from when the story arrives. Keep the tone satirical corporate-speak that slowly stops being funny. All final text goes through `t()` keys.

## How it grows
1. **Friendly (early).** A sponsor that appears in small places: a logo on a delivery crate, a "Synergy Partner" sticker in the bank app, the stock SYNR in the Investing preview. Pure flavour.
2. **Helpful (mid).** Offers appear: a bridge loan, a "wellness" grant, a free consultant. Terms are generous and slightly vague.
3. **Present (late).** They own a piece of everything, including your competitors and your suppliers. Their people turn up in meetings, at the permit board and in the council.
4. **Entangled (very late).** You are asked to take part. Refusing has a cost, accepting changes the rules of your own company.

## Beats that return from time to time
- A newsletter in LinkedOut, signed "The Synergy Family".
- A call or message from a polite stranger who knows how your week went.
- A stock tip in the bank app that always turns out to be right.
- A rival who suddenly gets a loan on friendly terms.
- A council vote that goes their way after "some lobbying".
- A competitor's pub that is quietly renamed after a Synergy slogan.

## Sample text
- "Synergy is not a company. Synergy is a feeling you have not had yet."
- "You are not a customer. You are a stakeholder in a larger us."
- "Your loan has been approved. No collateral needed, we trust you completely."
- "Our shareholders asked us to say hello. All of them. At once."
- "Synergy has taken a small, caring interest in your quarter."
- "We noticed you were thinking about leaving. Please don't think about that."
- "Welcome to the family. The family would like a word."
- "Congratulations on your promotion. It was already on the schedule."
- Bank notification: "Dividend received: Synergy Group (SYNR). You did not buy these shares. Isn't that nice?"
- Loan drawback examples: a board seat, a 'friendly' supplier you must use, a clause nobody can read.

## Mechanics that could fit later (undecided)
- Stocks everywhere: Synergy as a hidden shareholder in businesses, shown in an ownership view that reveals more as the story goes on.
- Synergy loans: cheaper than the bank, with a drawback that is a data modifier (forced supplier, reputation hit, lobbying cost).
- Lobbying: Synergy influences council votes and permits (ties in with the town and politics ideas in `docs/wishlist.md`).
- Involvement level: a story counter that gates new text, offers and finally a choice.
